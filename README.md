# i-tutor — Confidence in learning

Full-stack academic tuition platform built for **i-tutor** across **Student**, **Tutor**, and **Parent** portals, styled directly around the Warm Alabaster Cream (`#F6F1E7`), Matte Obsidian Charcoal (`#1E1F1D`), and Sunburst Gold (`#F2B728`) **i-tutor** brand identity.

## Core Pillars
1. **Role-Based Portals & Instant Switcher** — Student, Tutor Studio, and Parent Peace-of-Mind views plus live account registration and Night Study mode.
2. **Timetable, Class Scheduling & 1-Tap Attendance** — Group & 1-on-1 classes, 1-tap attendance register, Lesson Recaps, and 1-on-1 Extra-Help requests.
3. **Subjects & Structured Study Vault** — Formula sheets, worked examples, topic notes, and past-paper walkthroughs with bookmarking & mastery tracking.
4. **Interactive Auto-Graded Quizzes & Homework** — Timed quizzes with Socratic hints & worked explanations, plus homework submission and tutor grading.
5. **Sunrise Confidence Mastery Engine** — Live 0–100% Confidence Score per topic & subject with Weak-Spot Radar.
6. **24/7 `i-tutor` Socratic AI Study Coach** — Step-by-step equation solver, concept explainer, Socratic hint mode, and practice problem generator.
7. **Parent Progress View & Direct Messaging** — Live attendance, quiz/homework feedback, announcements, and Tutor-Parent / Tutor-Student messaging.
